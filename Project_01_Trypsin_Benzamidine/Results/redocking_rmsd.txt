REDOCKING RMSD ANALYSIS
========================================

Project: Molecular Docking and Redocking Validation of Benzamidine with Bovine Trypsin
Protein: Bovine Trypsin (PDB ID: 3PTB)
Ligand: Benzamidine
Docking Software: AutoDock Vina

1. OBJECTIVE
----------------------------------------
To evaluate the accuracy of the predicted benzamidine binding pose by comparing it with the experimentally observed benzamidine pose in the original 3PTB crystal structure.

2. RMSD CALCULATION DETAILS
----------------------------------------
Reference structure: Original crystal benzamidine (BEN) in 3PTB.pdb
Predicted structure: Best docking pose (Mode 1) from benzamidine_docked.pdbqt
Atoms considered: 9 corresponding heavy atoms
Atom mapping: Manually established between corresponding ligand atoms
Coordinate alignment: No additional alignment performed; both structures were compared in the original protein coordinate frame.

3. RMSD RESULT
----------------------------------------
Calculated RMSD: 0.3995 Å
Rounded RMSD: 0.40 Å

4. INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------
The calculated RMSD of approximately 0.40 Å indicates a close positional agreement between the predicted benzamidine binding pose and the experimentally observed crystal pose.

This result suggests that the docking protocol successfully reproduced the experimentally observed ligand orientation for this protein–ligand complex.

5. CONCLUSION
----------------------------------------
The low RMSD value supports the redocking performance of the docking workflow for the bovine trypsin–benzamidine complex.

Note: This RMSD represents the comparison with the experimental crystal ligand pose. It is different from the RMSD values reported by AutoDock Vina between its predicted docking modes.
