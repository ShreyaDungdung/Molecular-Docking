# Figure Guide

This document describes the molecular figures generated for the benzamidine–bovine trypsin docking project.

All molecular figures are generated from the structural data and analysis performed in this project.

## Figure 1. Benzamidine Chemical Structure

**File:** `figures/benzamidine_structure.png`

Shows the two-dimensional chemical representation of benzamidine prepared in its protonated form for docking.

**Purpose:** To illustrate the ligand used in the docking experiment.

## Figure 2. Trypsin Binding Pocket

**File:** `figures/trypsin_binding_pocket.png`

Shows the crystallographic benzamidine ligand within the binding pocket of bovine beta-trypsin.

**Purpose:** To visualize the ligand-binding region and surrounding amino acid residues.

## Figure 3. Crystal versus Docked Pose Comparison

**File:** `figures/crystal_vs_docked_v2.png`

Shows the crystallographic benzamidine pose and the best-ranked redocked pose in the protein binding pocket.

**Purpose:** To visually assess the agreement between the predicted and experimentally observed ligand orientations.

## Figure 4. Benzamidine Interaction with Asp189

**File:** `figures/asp189_interaction.png`

Shows the docked benzamidine ligand near the Asp189 side chain, with selected nitrogen–oxygen distances indicated.

**Purpose:** To illustrate the close polar contacts identified in the interaction analysis.

## Figure 5. Additional Crystal versus Docked Pose Comparison

**File:** `figures/crystal_vs_docked.png`

Provides an additional visualization of the crystallographic and redocked benzamidine poses.

**Purpose:** To provide an alternative view of ligand-pose agreement.

## Reproducibility

The corresponding PyMOL scripts and session files are included in the repository where available.

The PNG files are rendered visual outputs, while the PSE files preserve PyMOL visualization sessions for further inspection.
